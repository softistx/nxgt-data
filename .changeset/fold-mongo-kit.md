"@nxgt/mongo": minor

The MongoDB wiring of `@nxgt/mongo-kit` is now in this package. New exports: `defineMongo`, which checks a configuration of one or several databases and freezes it without connecting to anything, and `openMongo`, which opens the clients and gives back a `Mongo` whose `db` is the driver's own with every collection and every GridFS bucket typed on it — `mongo.db.users.create(…)`, `mongo.db.avatars.put(…)` — plus `as(actor)`, `withSession`, `transaction` (the collections and the buckets in the same session), `sync()`, `syncBuckets()`, `ping()` and a `close()` that gives back only the clients it opened. `discoverCollections` reads definitions from a glob for scripts, under Bun. Every refusal of the wiring is a `WiringError`, a `TypeError` with a `code` (`CONFIG`, `COLLISION`, `NO_DATABASE`, `SEVERAL_DATABASES`, `TRANSACTION`, `DERIVED`, `DISCOVERY`). The types come with them: `Mongo`, `MongoOf`, `MongoConfig`, `MongoConfigInput`, `MongoActor`, `MongoTransactionOptions`, `WiredCollectionOptions`, `WiredBucketOptions`, `WiringErrorCode`, `WiringErrorOptions`, `DbScope`, `SoleScope`, `DbName`, `DbMemberName`, `DatabaseConfig`, `CollectionsIn`, `CollectionsOf`, `BucketsIn`, `BucketsOf`, `BucketSyncReport`, `DiscoverOptions`, `NoCollision`, `NoBucketCollision`, `NoOwnedBucketOption`, `NoBucketsToOption` and `Unwired`.

It is what `@nxgt/mongo-kit` shipped, renamed: `createKit` is `openMongo`, `defineConfig` is `defineMongo`, `KitError` is `WiringError`, `MongoKit` is `Mongo`, `KitOf` is `MongoOf`, `KitConfig` and `KitConfigInput` are `MongoConfig` and `MongoConfigInput`, `KitActor` is `MongoActor`, `KitTransactionOptions` is `MongoTransactionOptions`, `KitBucketOptions` and `KitCollectionOptions` are `WiredBucketOptions` and `WiredCollectionOptions`, and `ReservedName` is `DbMemberName`. The messages follow: they begin `defineMongo:` and `openMongo:`, and the ones that named the object read `db: this Mongo has several databases. …`, `No database "…" in this Mongo: …` and `close: this Mongo came from … Close the one openMongo returned …`. The guides, the troubleshooting entries and the roadmap moved here with it. Nothing existing changes.
